AI for Business

Beyond the hype, AI creates measurable business value in a fairly small number of recurring patterns: predicting outcomes, automating repetitive decisions, understanding unstructured data, and personalizing at scale. Recognizing which pattern a problem fits is the first step to using AI well.

Each submodule below covers one pattern with a runnable version of the actual mechanism behind it — a real regression fit, a real threshold tradeoff, a real (if simplified) text classifier, a real recommender — not just a description of what these tools do.

  • Why forecasting/prediction is usually the lowest-risk, highest-ROI AI project
  • The tradeoff a threshold controls when automating a decision
  • How "AI reads your documents" reduces to classifying unstructured text
  • Why personalization means different inputs producing different outputs, mechanically
