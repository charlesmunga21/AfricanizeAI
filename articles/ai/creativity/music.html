Music Generation

AI music tools can generate backing tracks, suggest chord progressions, or produce full compositions from a text prompt or hummed melody, useful for scoring quick drafts, generating royalty-free background music, or breaking creative block by producing a starting point to react to.

"A starting point to react to"

This is the recurring theme across every creative medium in this module: the useful output usually isn't a finished piece, it's raw material a human then edits, rejects, or builds on. A generated chord progression that's "almost right" is often more useful than a blank page — it gives you something concrete to push against.

Visual model: generate a progression

Pick a mood, and get a 4-chord progression built for that mood. Regenerate for a different starting point within the same mood — a real tool works the same way: same intent, multiple candidate outputs, you pick or iterate.
