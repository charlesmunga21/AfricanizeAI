AI for Creativity

Generative AI models don't replace the creative decisions — what to make, for whom, and why — but they collapse the time between an idea and a rough draft of it, across writing, visual art, and music.

Each submodule pairs the concept with something you can actually run: a prompt-specificity tool that mirrors real LLM output quality, a step-through simulation of how diffusion models turn noise into an image, a mood-based chord generator, and an exercise that makes the limits of automation concrete rather than just asserted.
