Ask the Docs

A reading assistant for this site. Ask a question in plain language and it points you to the exact section that answers it — grounded in what's actually written here, not a general-purpose chatbot guessing.

Loads a small (~24 MB) embedding model and indexes every tutorial on this site — once. Both run in your browser; nothing you ask is sent anywhere.

How this works. Every tutorial page is broken into short passages and turned into a vector — a list of numbers that captures its meaning — by a small model (MiniLM) running on your device. Your question gets the same treatment, then the passages whose vectors are closest to your question's vector are shown, unedited, with a link to the real page. Nothing is generated or paraphrased, so there's nothing to hallucinate — you're always reading this site's actual words.
